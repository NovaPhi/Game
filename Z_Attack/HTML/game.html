How to Play

  • Objective Enter the green zone at the center of the enemy base to win the level.
  • Outposts Destroy all outposts before you can damage the main base walls.
  • Cards Win a level to draft a card. Use cards mid-run for powerful effects.
  • XP Earn XP every 3 levels. Spend it in the store to unlock new cards.
  • Difficulty Doubles every 3 levels — enemies shoot faster and spawn more.
